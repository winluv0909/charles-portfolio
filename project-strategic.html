Project Overview

Strategic International Manpower Services Inc. is a recruitment agency website developed to present company services, attract local and international applicants, and establish a professional online presence. The website focuses on responsive design, clean layouts, smooth animations, and an intuitive user experience.

Challenges

One of the main challenges was creating a modern recruitment website that looked professional while remaining lightweight and easy to navigate. Particular attention was given to responsive layouts, smooth scrolling, and ensuring the website worked consistently across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
